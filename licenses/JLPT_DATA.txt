Kotoba Offline - JLPT level metadata attribution

N5/N4 classification data is derived from Jonathan Waller's JLPT vocabulary data,
redistributed in structured form by stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab and compatible
projects such as OpenJLPT.

Jonathan Waller data: CC BY 4.0.
stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab redistribution: CC BY-SA 4.0.

The JLPT does not publish an official fixed vocabulary list for the current test.
Level labels in Kotoba are therefore indicative study metadata, not official JLPT
content specifications.

FreeDict Italian-Japanese remains the source of Italian-Japanese lexical entries.
