LEXICAL QA POLICY
FreeDict pairs are not treated as automatically correct. High-confidence source-pivot mismatches are corrected or merged only when supported by structured evidence from JMdict plus aligned Japanese/Italian WordNet concepts. Ambiguous conflicts are retained for later manual review rather than guessed.

GEOGRAPHIC PROPER-NAME POLICY
Proper names of countries, cities and comparable administrative subdivisions remain excluded from study. Version 2.7.6 extends the exclusion to continent names, named islands, archipelagos and explicit insular regions found in the study corpus. Stable vocabulary IDs are retained and the records are disabled rather than deleted, preserving upgrade/progress compatibility. Common geographic vocabulary (for example island, continent, country, city, mountain and river) and other natural geographic features are not removed solely for being geographic.
