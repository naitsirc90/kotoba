Kotoba Offline 3.1.7 - Didactic word selection priority

The ordering uses the frozen JMdict priority tags (nf / news / ichi / spec / gai)
and the sense-specific teaching signals already reviewed within Kotoba.
These tags are approximate relative-usage proxies, not official or contemporary
frequency counts. Words lacking frequency data are treated neutrally.
The index is included offline and never transmitted. No runtime AI is used.
All original JMdict and Kotoba 3.1.6 lexical attributions remain valid.
Source: EDRDG JMdict and the existing app corpus.
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

Kotoba key. It MUST NOT be treated as an installable update over 3.1.6.
